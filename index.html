Method

The tournament ran for 10 rounds. Each pairing uses four screened opening positions with colors swapped. Pair wins, draws and losses earn 1, 0.5 and 0 points. Ties use Buchholz then average successor count; remaining ties share rank. A bye earns one point and zero Buchholz contribution.

Bonus eligibility is limited to course participants who meet the competition qualification and review rules. Other ranked participants keep their final rank and do not consume bonus slots. The first 12 eligible participants receive 5 points, the next 12 receive 3 points, and the next 12 receive 1 point.

Each move has a 2,000-successor budget and a five-second response limit. The local final used gVisor isolation and the configured SDK counting mode.
